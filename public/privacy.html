PackLight privacy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

PackLight helps you organize personal responsibilities, emotions, resources, and goals. It is a reflection tool, not a medical service. Your entries are not monitored.

Data on your device

Your items, journals, mood and recovery ratings, history, and preferences are stored locally. PackLight has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or automatic cloud sync. The app does not request your contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Local storage is not encrypted separately by PackLight; protect access to your device.

Android app backups and device transfer are disabled for app data. On iOS, operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings. Browser storage can be erased by browser settings or eviction. Uninstalling may erase local data. Keep your own backup if you want to retain it.

Optional AI

AI is disabled until you supply your own Anthropic API key and accept the disclosure for the current app session. Selecting Save & Analyze sends the journal text and mood to Anthropic. Sending an agent chat sends your message and the names, descriptions, categories, weight, and utility of items linked to that reminder. Ordinary Save Entry does not use AI. PackLight does not automatically send your entire pack.

Requests go directly to Anthropic, which receives network information such as your IP address and processes requests under its terms. Anthropic API charges may apply to your account. Read Anthropic's privacy policy. PackLight cannot delete data already processed by Anthropic; contact Anthropic for those requests. AI output can be inaccurate. The API key is kept in app memory only and is not included in backups. Disable AI in Settings to remove it; it is also lost when the app process or page restarts.

Exports and deletion

Export Pack creates an unencrypted JSON file. On mobile, you select its destination with the system share sheet. Anyone with that file can read its contents. Temporary mobile export files are deleted after sharing completes; copies you choose to save or send remain with those destinations.

Clear All Data in Settings deletes this app's pack and key on this device. It does not delete your exported files, OS backups, or information already sent to Anthropic. No PackLight server copy exists.

Support

For support or privacy questions, use the PackLight issue tracker. It is public: do not post journals, backup files, API keys, or other sensitive information. Opening support or other external links is subject to that service's privacy policy.
